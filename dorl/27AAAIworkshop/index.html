Sources, interpretation & coverage

What was checked

Checked on October 17, 2026. The inventory comes from the official AAAI-27 workshop program list. Every listed workshop link was checked against the shared dataset. Edition-specific CFP pages were followed where available; Pages requiring JavaScript rendering are checked in a browser when needed. Missing, inaccessible, and unannounced workshop dates remain Unknown. General AAAI dates and reference-only dates are retained only in source notes; they never fill an Unknown field.

The program overview supplies the general November 20 / December 2 baseline and permits workshop exceptions. The organizer timetable uses AoE (UTC−12), but individual sites may specify another zone or omit the cutoff. Per-workshop missing zones are marked Unknown.

How to read the dates

  • Regular paper deadlines appear in the two main date columns. Separate fast tracks appear inside evidence notes.
  • Current workshop CFP dates take precedence over the general baseline. Social Simulation and EAIM conflict with their individual AAAI listings; both versions are recorded.
  • “Both dates on workshop websites” counts only dates explicitly found on the current workshop sites. Dates available only in AAAI’s list, missing dates, inaccessible sources, and unannounced dates are not treated as confirmed workshop dates.
  • Old-edition dates, reviewer deadlines, registration dates, and camera-ready dates are excluded from the main columns.
  • This is a dated research snapshot. Source links let you check for later changes before submitting.
