About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at the University of Toronto, supervised by Prof. Chi-Guhn Lee in the Dynamic Optimization and Reinforcement Learning (DORL) Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of robotics, reinforcement learning, and simulation to reality. Combining physical intuition from mechanics with modern RL, I am passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical control principles and data-driven approaches.

Research

My current research focuses on three closely connected directions.

Robot simulation
Developing custom, physics-rich environments in NVIDIA Isaac Sim to bridge the sim-to-real gap.
Offline skill discovery
Leveraging offline datasets to extract reusable primitive skills for long-horizon planning tasks.
RL systems optimization
Enhancing computational efficiency through structural innovations in grouping, scheduling, and communication architectures.
